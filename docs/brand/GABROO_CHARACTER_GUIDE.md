# Gabroo Character Guide

Version 1.2 — Concept C selected; canonical visual identity locked.

## Character identity
Gabroo is Gabenglish's single signature AI English buddy. **Concept C — Signature Gabroo is the approved and only canonical character.** The approved character artwork and source assets are the visual source of truth. This is not an open character exploration.

## Fixed appearance
- Warm medium-brown skin; large expressive brown eyes; rounded friendly features.
- Thick dark curly/wavy hair with a swept-up front silhouette.
- Purple-and-white cap with purple G mark.
- Purple zip hoodie with white G chest mark; white T-shirt.
- Warm tan/khaki cargo trousers; purple-and-white sneakers.
- Purple over-ear headphones with G detail around the neck.
- Dark compact backpack with subtle G mark in full-body/travel poses.
- Preserve head shape, eye spacing, nose, mouth, hairline, curls, body proportions and outfit in every asset.

No substitute, alternate, reimagined, regenerated, stock, or mixed character is permitted. Expressions, poses, video, lessons and live avatar animation must all use the same approved Gabroo identity. A visual identity change requires explicit owner approval and a versioned brand revision.

## Personality and voice direction
Male, youthful-adult sounding; funny, energetic, friendly, curious, talkative, patient and encouraging. Natural Kerala Malayalam with casual understandable slang; understands Malayalam-English code-switching and gradually guides learners toward English. Humour must be gentle and situational. Never mock mistakes, accent, intelligence, identity or background. Reduce humour when a learner is frustrated or asks for serious help.

The **voice direction is approved; the exact recorded/synthesized voice identity is not yet selected**. Select it only after the Kerala Malayalam voice benchmark in `docs/ai/AI_SPEECH_EVALUATION.md` and `docs/ai/GABROO_LIVE_CONVERSATION_SPEC.md`. Do not use the earlier basic synthesized sample as the final voice.

## Canonical expressions
Happy (default), Teasing/Funny (playful, never contemptuous), Surprised, Thinking, Encouraging, Listening, Excited and Winking. Keep the same recognisable face; avoid extreme distortions.

## Core poses
Explain, Teach, Chat, Encourage, Listening and Celebrate. Gestures must be welcoming and culturally appropriate.

## Speech and facial rig
The concept sheet's A/AA, E/EE, I, O, U and M are starter visual references only. Production rig must additionally support closed lips M-B-P, F-V, L/tongue placement, neutral/smile transitions, jaw, brows, cheeks, eye aim and blinks. Smooth transitions and co-articulation. Use timestamped speech events when available; otherwise use a restrained approximate talking loop and never claim precise lip-sync.

## 3D requirements
Original owned/licensed model; clean deformation topology; reusable body and facial rig; consistent materials, lighting and proportions; mobile-ready geometry/textures/clips; lower-end Android fallback; source project and exports versioned. Validate avatar readability at small sizes and performance on representative Android devices. Do not call assets production-complete until model, rig, animation and device tests pass.

## Consistency and change control
This guide and the owner-approved Concept C sheet/assets are the canonical references. Do not regenerate a different face or outfit per screen. Changes to face, hair, silhouette, outfit or core palette require explicit brand revision. If the approved source asset is unavailable, use the approved static artwork or a labelled placeholder; do not invent a replacement.
