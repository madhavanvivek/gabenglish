# Gabenglish Curriculum Framework

Version 1.0 — curriculum architecture; detailed lesson authoring pending.

## 1. Learning philosophy
Teach usable English from a Malayalam support base. Start with meaning and practical use, build sentence patterns and confidence, then reduce Malayalam assistance as evidence of comprehension and independent expression improves. Learners can request Malayalam at every stage.

## 2. Five stages
1. Malayalam Mode: words, meanings, pronunciation and basic sentences.
2. Malayalam + English Mix: everyday grammar and sentence formation.
3. English Practice Mode: conversation, listening and expression.
4. English Immersion: natural social and workplace English.
5. Expert Communication: advanced, professional and spontaneous communication.

## 3. Learning outcomes
Stage 1: recognise/use common words, introduce self, form basic statements/questions and short supported exchanges.
Stage 2: construct everyday sentences, describe routines and past/future events, ask questions and translate common expressions naturally.
Stage 3: sustain simple conversations, narrate events, understand common spoken exchanges and self-correct frequent errors.
Stage 4: participate in longer social/workplace interactions, express opinions, understand natural expressions and adapt register.
Stage 5: communicate nuanced ideas, present, persuade, debate, interview and handle professional communication clearly.

## 4. Curriculum domains
Sounds/pronunciation foundations; vocabulary/collocations; sentence construction; parts of speech; tenses/aspect; questions/negatives; articles/prepositions; modals/conditionals; active/passive; reported speech; complex sentences; listening; everyday conversation; storytelling; social/workplace communication; interviews/presentations; idioms, phrasal verbs and register.

## 5. Word-first teaching pattern
English word; part of speech; Malayalam meaning; pronunciation audio; simple Malayalam explanation; example sentences; collocations; similar/confused words; common mistakes; learner-generated sentence; retrieval/revision.

## 6. Lesson schema
Stable content ID/version; stage/prerequisites; title/objective; target vocabulary/grammar; Malayalam explanation; English examples; audio/transcripts; exercises/hints; accepted answers/evaluation rubric; common errors/feedback; completion criteria; author/reviewer; publication status.

## 7. Assessment/progression
Assess recognition, recall, sentence construction, listening, guided speaking, independent speaking and retention separately. Stage advancement requires multiple evidence points and confidence check; explain why next stage is recommended.

## 8. Spaced repetition
Schedule vocabulary/concepts based on recall and time since review. Allow manual review. Do not punish missed days or make streak loss a learning barrier.

## 9. Editorial QA
Malayalam reviewed by fluent Kerala Malayalam editors; English reviewed for grammar/naturalness; alternative answers documented; audio checked; age/cultural suitability reviewed; content versioned; exercises tested before release.
